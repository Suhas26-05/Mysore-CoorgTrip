01 — Overview

The Trip Frame

Two overnight buses bookend four full days. Everything in between runs on a hired Tempo Traveller — public transport will not hold this schedule together.

WhenWhat
Sep 30 · 7:30 PMBoard overnight sleeper at MGBS / LB Nagar → Mysuru (~13 hrs)
Oct 1 · ThuMysuru city — palace, market, 1,008 steps, musical fountain
Oct 2 · FriMysuru → Coorg via Dubare rafting, Nisargadhama & Bylakuppe
Oct 3 · SatCoorg — sunrise off-road, waterfalls, plantation trek
Oct 4 · SunCoorg → Mysuru via Srirangapatna. Board 7:30 PM bus
Oct 5 · MonHyderabad by ~8:00 AM

Ground Transport

Tempo Traveller for all four days, ₹18,000–24,000 total. Book a driver familiar with Kodagu ghat roads.

Why Early October

  • Waterfalls at peak flow
  • Reservoirs full, everything green
  • Cool 15–26°C in the hills
  • Short afternoon showers possible

Stay Strategy

Night 1 in Mysuru near KR Circle. Nights 2–3 in a coffee estate homestay, not a hotel — it changes the entire character of the trip.

Timings are deliberately tight in the mornings. The one rule that matters: leave when the plan says leave.

Mysuru, On Foot

Neo-Gothic twin spires, best photographed at dusk.

Trade the cathedral for go-karting in Mysuru if the group wants adrenaline over architecture.

River RaftingElephant CampMonastery

Depart Mysuru

8:00 PM

Replace the entire morning with the Tadiandamol trek — Coorg's highest peak at 1,748 m, ~7 km round trip, 4–5 hours. Start by 6:30 AM. Expect deep mud and leeches in October.

03 — The Social Plan

Coffee, Not Cocktails

Coorg grows a third of India's coffee. For a group that doesn't drink, that is a far better ritual than any bar — and you'll be awake at 5 AM anyway.

Estate Cupping

Most plantations run a formal tasting — grind, brew, slurp, score. Arabica versus Robusta becomes an actual argument by the end of it.

Madikeri Cafés

  • Coorg Coffee Cafe
  • Tiger Tiger
  • Raintree

Take Some Home

Buy green or freshly roasted beans directly from the estate. Cheaper than any city roaster and the best souvenir from this trip.

Bonfire Nights

Two of them. Music, cards, antakshari, ghost stories. This is where the trip actually happens.

Eat Kodava

  • Pandi curry
  • Kadambuttu
  • Akki roti
  • Bamboo shoot curry

Note on Oct 2

Gandhi Jayanti — a nationwide dry day. Irrelevant for this group, and it means quieter viewpoints.

06 — Before You Go

Booking & Verification

Oct 2–4 is a long weekend. The first three items below are genuinely urgent.

0 of 0 done

Confirm these a few days before — they change week to week

  • Dubare rafting operation for early October — water levels vary
  • Mandalpatti jeep permits, occasionally suspended after heavy rain
  • Abbey Falls viewing-deck access if flow is extreme
  • Ranganathittu boating status on Oct 4
  • Mysuru Palace illumination timings — usually Sunday evenings, 7:00–7:45 PM

A single phone call to your homestay host two days out will confirm most of this list.
