01 — Overview

The Trip Frame

Two overnight bus journeys bookend four days on the ground. The route now runs Mysuru arrival → Kushalnagar → Coorg → Kukke → Mysuru before the return to Hyderabad.

WhenWhat
Sep 30 · 7:30 PMBoard overnight sleeper at MGBS / LB Nagar → Mysuru
Oct 1 · Thu · Day 1Mysuru arrival → Kushalnagar sightseeing → Coorg
Oct 2 · Fri · Day 2Full Coorg day — Mandalpatti, Abbey Falls, coffee estate, Madikeri & Raja's Seat
Oct 3 · Sat · Day 3Coorg → Kukke — Kukke Subramanya, Kumaradhara and nearby attractions
Oct 4 · Sun · Day 4Kukke → Mysuru — Mysuru sightseeing, shopping, dinner & 7:30 PM Hyderabad bus
Oct 5 · Mon · Day 5Arrive Hyderabad by roughly 8:00 AM

Ground Transport

Tempo Traveller for the road days, with a driver familiar with Kodagu and Western Ghats roads. Keep the vehicle with the group through the final Mysuru transfer.

Why This Route

  • Mysuru is the arrival point, not Day 1 sightseeing
  • Kushalnagar gets a dedicated route day
  • Coorg gets a full day
  • Kukke gets its own main day
  • Mysuru is saved for the final day

Stay Strategy

Night 1 in Coorg, night 2 in Coorg, night 3 in Kukke. No unnecessary return to Mysuru until Day 4.

03 — The Social Plan

Coffee, Not Cocktails

Coorg grows a third of India's coffee. For a group that doesn't drink, that is a far better ritual than any bar — and you'll be awake at 5 AM anyway.

Estate Cupping

Most plantations run a formal tasting — grind, brew, slurp, score. Arabica versus Robusta becomes an actual argument by the end of it.

Madikeri Cafés

  • Coorg Coffee Cafe
  • Tiger Tiger
  • Raintree

Take Some Home

Buy green or freshly roasted beans directly from the estate. Cheaper than any city roaster and the best souvenir from this trip.

Bonfire Nights

Two of them. Music, cards, antakshari, ghost stories. This is where the trip actually happens.

Eat Kodava

  • Pandi curry
  • Kadambuttu
  • Akki roti
  • Bamboo shoot curry

Note on Oct 2

Gandhi Jayanti — a nationwide dry day. Irrelevant for this group, and it means quieter viewpoints.

06 — Before You Go

Booking & Verification

Oct 2–4 is a long weekend. The first three items below are genuinely urgent.

0 of 0 done

Confirm these a few days before — they change week to week

  • Dubare rafting operation for early October — water levels vary
  • Mandalpatti jeep permits, occasionally suspended after heavy rain
  • Abbey Falls viewing-deck access if flow is extreme
  • Ranganathittu boating status on Oct 4
